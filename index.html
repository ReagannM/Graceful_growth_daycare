What are your opening hours?expand_more

We are open Monday to Saturday, from 6:00 am to 6:00 pm.

Where are you located?expand_more

You'll find us at Greatwall Gardens, Phase 1, Gate B, Block 30.

Do you offer flexible childcare?expand_more

We offer hourly and daily childcare options. Call or email us to discuss your schedule and what might work for your family.
