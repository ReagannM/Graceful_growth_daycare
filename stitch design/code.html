Clear & Honest Answers

Questions Parents Often Ask

Everything you need to know about our daily routine, nutrition, hygiene, and enrollment.

What ages do you accept at Graceful Growth? expand_more
We welcome children aged 2 to 7 years. Our groups are organized thoughtfully into Little Explorers (2–3 yrs), Curious Minds (4–5 yrs), and Confident Beginnings (6–7 yrs) so each child interacts with peers at an aligned developmental level.
What are your standard opening and closing hours? expand_more
Our standard daycare operations run Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. This schedule is designed specifically to accommodate parents commuting into Nairobi, Machakos, or surrounding industrial zones.
Where exactly are you located in Greatwall Gardens? expand_more
We are conveniently situated inside Greatwall Gardens Phase 2, Athi River / Mavoko area. It is a peaceful, double-gated community with 24/7 security and lush green courtyard playgrounds. Detailed building directions are shared upon booking your visit.
Do you provide freshly prepared meals? expand_more
Yes! We prepare wholesome, warm, balanced meals on-site every day. This includes morning uji/porridge or milk with healthy carbs, mid-morning fruit slices, a hearty lunch (such as rice, steamed vegetables, lentils, beans, or tender stew), and an afternoon energy snack.
Do children have an afternoon nap or rest period? expand_more
Yes. Between 1:30 PM and 3:00 PM, we have a quiet rest period in our sanitized, low-lit nap room with individual resting mattresses. Children who do not sleep are engaged in calming table tasks such as soft coloring or picture books without disturbing sleeping peers.
What items should my child bring on their first day? expand_more
A backpack with two labeled complete changes of weather-appropriate clothes, comfortable indoor socks or slippers, a labeled water bottle, and a familiar comfort item (like a small blanket) if they need it for nap time.
How do you handle toilet training for young toddlers? expand_more
We partner with parents with tenderness and patience. We introduce regular, cheerful potty pauses with zero pressure or punishment. Accidents are handled calmly and matter-of-factly to preserve the child's self-esteem.
How do teachers communicate with parents during the day? expand_more
We provide a detailed verbal debrief at pickup covering meals, nap duration, and highlights of the day. In addition, we maintain a secure private WhatsApp line for photos, urgent messages, or any questions you may have during working hours.
Can I visit and observe before confirming enrollment? expand_more
We strongly encourage it! Seeing your child smile in our classrooms and meeting our teachers face-to-face gives you complete clarity. You can book an unhurried morning or afternoon tour using our visit form below.
Do you offer extended care for unusual work hours? expand_more
Yes. By prior agreement, early drop-offs starting from 5:30 AM or late pickups up to 7:00 PM can be arranged to support parents on night shifts or those navigating heavy traffic.
How do you manage allergies or special dietary restrictions? expand_more
All dietary restrictions, sensitivities, or lactose intolerances are recorded in your child's primary profile and displayed in our food preparation kitchen. Our cooks strictly customize meals or serve parent-provided meals with precision.
What happens if my child feels unwell during the day? expand_more
We have a quiet, comfortable wellness corner where a teacher stays with your child to provide gentle hydration and rest. We contact parents immediately to inform you and coordinate appropriate care or pickup according to first-aid protocol.
