## Brand & Style

The brand personality is rooted in heartfelt community care, emotional safety, and organic childhood development. It speaks directly to hardworking Kenyan parents living in and around Greatwall Gardens, offering absolute reassurance, transparent communication, and genuine warmth. The interface should feel like stepping into a sunlit, peaceful courtyard filled with greenery, natural timber, and gentle guidance—not an impersonal corporate institution or an overstimulating neon playground.

The visual direction combines **Human-Crafted Tactility** with **Calm Modern Warmth**. Visual weight is grounded through organic curves, soft earth-tinted surfaces, and spacious breathing room. Micro-interactions should feel leisurely and forgiving rather than rigid and mechanical. Above all, the interface evokes peace of mind, high accountability, and the affectionate spirit of home.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from organic Kenyan clay, lush domestic flora, and golden sunbeams, completely rejecting harsh digital primaries or sterile clinic blues.

- **Primary (`#4A6B53` — Sage Foliage):** The grounding anchor. Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and high-trust framing. A deeper companion shade (`#3B5742`) is reserved for hover/pressed states.
- **Secondary (`#D97757` — Sunbaked Terracotta):** Emotive, nurturing, and inviting. Applied to conversion moments, milestone highlights, warm alerts, and playful interactive touches.
- **Tertiary (`#F3CA65` — Soft Honey Gold):** Used sparingly for celebratory badges, daily activity tags (e.g., naptime, outdoor play), and gentle informational callouts.
- **Neutral Core (`#2C2523` — Rich Roast Brown):** Replaces pure black to ensure high contrast without mechanical severity. Secondary typography rests in `#443B38`.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: Warm Linen Cream (`#FAF7F2`)
  - Elevated Container / Card Background: Soft Ivory Sand (`#F5F0E6`)
  - Elevated Popover / Modal Background: `#FFFFFF` tinted with 1% warm ochre
  - Hairline Separators: `#E8E0D2`

## Typography

The type system blends the grounded, handcrafted charm of **Epilogue** for display and headline titles with the open, friendly clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, captions, and narrative copy.

- Display and headline titles employ soft weight variations (500 and 600) rather than heavy ultra-bold cuts to keep headers welcoming and gentle.
- Body text is set with generous line heights (1.5x minimum) to reduce cognitive fatigue for parents checking updates during transit or busy workdays.
- Numerical data (e.g., arrival times, age tiers, fee breakdowns) retains comfortable tabular spacing using Plus Jakarta Sans with proportional figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a relaxed fluid grid anchored inside structured containment bounds. Layouts prioritize clarity, uncluttered vertical rhythm, and ample negative space to maintain a stress-free environment.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `1.25rem` column gutters. Components stack linearly into full-width cards with `space-md` internal padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates two-column splits (e.g., attendance records alongside daily menus).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at an inner maximum container width of `1200px` to avoid overstretched reading lengths.
- Vertical section spacing scales strictly with `space-xl` and higher multiples to maintain calm separation between daily logs, photo feeds, and administrative modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows and neon glow filters in favor of **Tonal Nesting** paired with warm, diffuse **Ambient Earth Shadows**.

- **Base Layer:** Canvas sits at Warm Linen Cream (`#FAF7F2`).
- **Surface Elevation (Cards, Panels):** Ivory Sand (`#F5F0E6`) framed with subtle organic outlines (`1px solid #E8E0D2`). No drop shadow is required at resting state.
- **Interactive Hover & Lifted State:** Elements scale slightly (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) supported by a warm, sunlit shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 43, 35, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 43, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Modals & Drawers:** High surface white (`#FFFFFF`) with a rich backdrop overlay (`#2C2523` at 40% opacity) and an extended ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(44, 37, 35, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses gentle warmth through smooth, rounded contours that evoke natural stones and handcrafted wooden blocks. 

- Interactive controls, alerts, and input wrappers utilize `rounded` corners (`0.5rem / 8px`).
- Structural containers, curriculum modules, and photo cards employ `rounded-lg` (`1rem / 16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem / 24px`).
- Filter tags, status indicators, and quick-dial buttons use pill geometries (`rounded-full`) to communicate immediate, friendly tapability.
- Sharp `90-degree` corners are prohibited across all interactive and content boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Sage Green (`#4A6B53`) with white text, `rounded` corners, and generous horizontal padding (`0.75rem 1.5rem`). Hover transitions smoothly to `#3B5742`.
- **Secondary / Milestone:** Filled in Terracotta (`#D97757`) with white text for enrollment actions and emergency call triggers. Hover shifts to `#C66343`.
- **Tonal / Ghost:** Tinted surface (`rgba(74, 107, 83, 0.08)`) with Sage Green text and no structural border.

### Cards & Content Containers
- Background in Warm Sand (`#F5F0E6`) bounded by an organic border (`1px solid #E8E0D2`) and `rounded-xl` curvature.
- Headers integrate child profile tags alongside clean metadata stamps in muted roast brown.

### Chips & Activity Badges
- Soft status pills styled with tinted pastel fills:
  - Outdoor Play / Discovery: Honey Yellow (`#F3CA65` at 25% tint with `#443B38` text)
  - Nutrition / Meals: Terracotta (`#D97757` at 18% tint with `#2C2523` text)
  - Rest / Wellness: Sage Green (`#4A6B53` at 18% tint with `#2C2523` text)

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background is crisp tinted ivory (`#FCFAF7`) with an inset border of `#DED7C8` and `0.5rem` border radius.
- Focus states employ a dual-ring: an inner crisp stroke of `#4A6B53` with a diffuse outer glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(74, 107, 83, 0.2)`). Error states substitute Warm Terracotta.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom rounded boxes (`6px` for checkbox, circular for radio) framed in `#C8C0B2`. Active check utilizes Sage Green background with a warm cream check glyph.

### Daycare-Specific Modules
- **Daily Reflection Timeline:** Soft vertical continuous line in `#E8E0D2` interrupted by rounded icon nodes denoting drop-off, mid-day meals, creative learning, and pickup readiness.
- **Teacher Handover Note:** Pinned card featuring a warm honey highlight border and cursive micro-label for notes between parents and educators.
